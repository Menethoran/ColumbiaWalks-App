Open CBPD’s official tip form ↗

Opens in a new tab. This website cannot autofill or close prompts inside a separate CRIMEWATCH tab. The app’s embedded form provides those conveniences.

  1. Choose anonymity and Other

    Select “I wish to remain anonymous” and Other as the crime type. If the subscription popup appears in your browser, choose “No thanks, just browsing.”

  2. Paste and review

    Paste the prepared subject and message into their matching fields. Keep the [TEST] markings when editing. Attach any relevant original files yourself; attachments can contain identifying information.

  3. Personally submit

    Read and accept the required agreement, complete any CAPTCHA, and press Submit yourself. Read CRIMEWATCH’s response to determine whether it received your tip. Opening this link does not send a tip. Filling the form does not confirm receipt.
