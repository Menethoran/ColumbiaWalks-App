Emergency or a crime in progress?

Call 911 now. Online tips are for non-emergency information.

Submit directly to CBPD

Continue to Columbia Borough Police Department’s CRIMEWATCH page to share information about suspicious, nuisance, or criminal activity. The official form lets you choose whether to include contact information.

Open CBPD’s official tip form ↗

Opens the Police Department’s CRIMEWATCH form in a new tab.

  1. Choose anonymity

    Under Anonymity, select “I wish to remain anonymous.” Avoid identifying yourself in your message or attachments if you want to remain anonymous.

  2. Add what you know

    Choose the type of issue, then enter a subject and message. Include the location, timing, and relevant details you observed. Add any supporting files on the official form.

  3. Review and submit on CRIMEWATCH

    Read the form’s required agreement, complete its verification steps, and press its Submit button yourself. Opening the form does not send a tip.

Your tip goes through the official form

This ColumbiaWalks page does not collect tip text, contact details, or attachments. No ColumbiaWalks report is automatically forwarded when you open the link. CBPD and CRIMEWATCH handle information entered on their site under their own privacy practices; ColumbiaWalks cannot confirm receipt.
