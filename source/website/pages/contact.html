Need to reach the police?

For emergencies or a crime in progress, call 911. ColumbiaWalks email and phone are for the community project and are not police reporting channels.

For a non-emergency tip, use Columbia Borough Police Department’s official anonymous-tip form. Choose the anonymous option on CRIMEWATCH and submit there.
