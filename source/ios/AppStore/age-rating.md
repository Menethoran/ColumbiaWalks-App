# Age Rating questionnaire

Recommended storefront rating: **4+**, subject to App Store Connect's generated result.

- Parental controls: None
- Age assurance: None
- Unrestricted web access: No
- Gambling or contests: None
- Loot boxes: None
- In-app purchases: None
- Advertising: None
- Messaging or chat: None
- User-generated content visible inside the app: None
- Social networking: None
- Alcohol, tobacco, cannabis, guns, profanity, horror, sexual content, or medical treatment information: None
- Violence: None

The app accepts safety-report text and photos and trash-can text for administrator review but does not present a public user-content feed inside the app. Photos are optional for standard reports and required for Repeat and Page of Shame reports. A Page of Shame picture or an approved/redacted public trash-can comment can appear on ColumbiaWalks public services only after administrator review. Private can complaints are not published. The app can open the external official Police Department tip form but does not embed an unrestricted browser, submit a tip automatically, provide medical advice, or dispatch emergency services. It directs emergencies and incidents in progress to 911.
