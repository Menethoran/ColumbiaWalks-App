Trash reports now include a hauler and Residential/Commercial, defaulting to Residential. Repeat Reporting adds trash cans and keeps your issue, hauler, property type, and property scope selected for the next can. Each repeat report needs its own photo and confirmed location and is saved as a private complaint to ColumbiaWalks.

Police-tip preparation carries complaint details into Columbia Borough Police Department's official CRIMEWATCH form, selects Anonymous and Other, and keeps mandatory [TEST] labels in the prepared subject and message. You review the form, choose attachments, complete the agreement and CAPTCHA, and submit it yourself. The app never submits the official form or claims police receipt.
