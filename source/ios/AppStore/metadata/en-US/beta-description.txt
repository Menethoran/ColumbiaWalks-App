ColumbiaWalks 3.16.0 is an independent community pedestrian-safety app for Columbia, Pennsylvania. Testers can submit guided or Repeat reports, use picture GPS with manual override, review the offline queue, and use a new Community hub without creating an account. Community prepares a local-only draft for a past, non-active anonymous police tip before an explicit handoff to the official Police Department site; ColumbiaWalks does not receive that draft or evidence. It also separates moderated public-trash-can comments from private can complaints that are not automatically forwarded to the Borough, while retaining App Feedback. The limited server-generated report-email field test remains restricted to an exact single crosswalk or missing-sidewalk selection within 5 km and only the ColumbiaWalks-controlled test mailbox with `[TEST]` in the subject—not Police, the Mayor, or Codes. Authorization does not confirm delivery.
