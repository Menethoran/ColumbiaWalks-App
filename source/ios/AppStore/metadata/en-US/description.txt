ColumbiaWalks helps residents and visitors document pedestrian-safety concerns in Columbia, Pennsylvania.

Choose a point on the map, use your current location, or use GPS embedded in a picture. You can review or manually override coordinates before submitting. Reports are saved on your device first and submitted when a connection is available.

Repeat Reporting keeps a sidewalk, vehicle, crossing, visibility, accessibility, school-route, police-response, or other hierarchy ready while you walk. Take or select a picture, optionally record a sidewalk subtype, lip height, vehicle behavior, license plate/state, or comment, submit, and immediately prepare the next observation. A separate Page of Shame flow sends a picture and location for administrator review before any public use.

For two narrowly defined concerns within 5 km of Columbia Borough center, the app shows a clear disclosure before submission and can authorize the ColumbiaWalks server to process a field-test email after successful upload. Only an exact single crosswalk encroachment/incursion or missing-sidewalk selection qualifies; mixed, duplicate, and hierarchy-stray selections remain ordinary reports. In version 3.16, every authorized message goes only to a ColumbiaWalks-controlled test mailbox with `[TEST]` in the subject—not to Police, the Mayor, or Codes. If processed, the message includes the submitted picture, confirmed location, and details, plus a prominently identified optional plate/state for a crosswalk report. Authorization does not confirm delivery, and server delivery safeguards apply. Other report types are not automatically emailed. Email is sent from the ColumbiaWalks account, never from your personal email account.

The Saved tab lets you review pending and submitted reports, retry a submission, share a report summary, or remove a local copy.

The Community tab adds three clearly separated tools. Prepare a structured, local-only draft for a past, non-active anonymous police tip, then choose whether to continue on the Columbia Borough Police Department's official site. ColumbiaWalks does not receive the draft or evidence; attachment, attestation, reCAPTCHA, and final submission happen on the official external form. You can also send a moderated comment about a public trash can or a separate private can complaint. Can complaints are not published or automatically forwarded to the Borough. App feedback remains available with optional contact information. No account is required.

ColumbiaWalks supports community awareness and advocacy. It does not contact emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical help, call 911.

ColumbiaWalks is independently operated. It does not represent or act on behalf of a government entity. Version 3.16 does not automatically share reports with local officials; its limited, visibly disclosed email field test uses only a ColumbiaWalks-controlled mailbox.
