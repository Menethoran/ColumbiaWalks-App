ColumbiaWalks 3.16.0 (31600) needs no account, payment, invitation, or activation. Its five tabs are Map, Report, Repeat, Saved, and Community. Location permission is optional.

Suggested review:
1. On Map, place a pin near Columbia, Pennsylvania, or use the current location.
2. In Report, Quick Report can normally be saved without a category or location. Full Report shows all issue types. Selecting only Crosswalk encroachment or only Missing sidewalk shows a persistent field-test email disclosure and requires a relevant picture and confirmed location within 5 km. The disclosure says any eligible server-generated message goes only to a ColumbiaWalks-controlled test mailbox with [TEST] in its subject, never Police, the Mayor, or Codes. Adding a second Quick type removes that authorization. Page of Shame separately discloses administrator review before possible public use.
3. Repeat keeps the selected hierarchy ready while per-event picture, comment, location, and vehicle identifiers reset. Missing sidewalk and Crosswalk incursion show the same limited test-mailbox disclosure when their exact eligibility rules are met. Camera and library pickers are cancellable.
4. Saved shows local queue/submission state. Report acceptance and email status are separate; neither claims guaranteed delivery.
5. Community has three distinct destinations:
   - Prepare an Anonymous Police Tip creates and copies a local-only draft for a past, non-active matter. It sends nothing to ColumbiaWalks. Opening the official Police Department site is not submission; the user must select anonymous, paste, attach evidence, personally attest, complete reCAPTCHA, and submit there. Please do not file a sample police tip during review.
   - Trash Cans separates moderated public comments from private complaints. Public comments are locked to public trash cans and may be published only after review/redaction. Private complaints are not published or automatically forwarded to the Borough. Both require a written location; a confirmed map pin is optional. Version 3.16 queues JSON and attaches no trash-can media.
   - App Feedback remains anonymous by default; optional contact fields require a separate consent choice.

The app has no public in-app user-content feed, messaging, ads, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party login. It is an independent community pedestrian-safety tool, not a government, police, emergency, medical, legal, or financial service. Emergencies and incidents in progress are directed to 911.

Core services are Apple MapKit, Core Location, PhotosUI, UIPasteboard, camera access, and ColumbiaWalks HTTPS endpoints at directus.rndtech.org/columbiawalks-api. The police-tip tool opens the official CRIMEWATCH site externally and has no ColumbiaWalks police-upload path. No Gmail credential or recipient address is in the app.

After upload, the server may request model-derived incident weather from Open-Meteo using only two-decimal incident coordinates and the incident date/hour. It sends no live phone location, precise coordinates, narrative, photo, contact, or report ID. Estimates and provenance may be stored; Open-Meteo is attributed under CC BY 4.0. The app contacts no weather service, adds no permission, and weather failure never blocks a report.
