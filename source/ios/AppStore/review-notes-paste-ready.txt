ColumbiaWalks 3.16.1 (31601) needs no account, payment, invitation, or activation. Its five tabs are Map, Report, Repeat, Saved, and Community. Location permission is optional.

Suggested review:
1. On Map, place a pin near Columbia, Pennsylvania, or use current location.
2. In Report, Quick Report can be saved without a category, location, or photo. Location and identification start collapsed. The two actions are Submit complaint to CW and Submit to CW & Notify CBPD. The second saves the CW report, then opens a prepared anonymous-tip draft; ColumbiaWalks does not send it or media to CBPD. The user must finish the official form.
3. Selecting only Crosswalk encroachment or only Missing sidewalk reveals a collapsed test-email control that is off by default. It can be enabled only with a relevant photo and confirmed location within 5 km, but those items remain optional for the CW report. Any opted-in server message goes only to a ColumbiaWalks-controlled test mailbox with [TEST] in its subject, not to Police, the Mayor, or Codes. Adding a second Quick type removes the control. Authorization does not confirm delivery.
4. Repeat requires its own picture and location and keeps the selected hierarchy ready while per-event fields reset. Missing sidewalk and Crosswalk incursion show the same off-by-default test-email control. Camera and library pickers are cancellable.
5. Saved shows local queue/submission state. Report acceptance and test-email state are separate; neither claims guaranteed delivery.
6. Community includes:
   - Contact Us, listing Robert at (717) 466-9069 with Call, Text, and private app/project feedback paths.
   - Prepare an Anonymous Police Tip for a past, non-active matter. A standalone draft stays local. A draft prepared after a CW report clearly states that the report was saved to CW but nothing was sent to CBPD. Opening the official site is not submission; the user must select anonymous, paste, attach evidence, personally attest, complete reCAPTCHA, and submit there. Please do not file a sample police tip during review.
   - Trash Cans, separating moderated public comments from private complaints. Private complaints are not published or automatically forwarded to the Borough.
   - App Feedback, anonymous by default with optional contact and separate follow-up consent.

The app has no public in-app user-content feed, messaging, ads, analytics, tracking, purchases, or third-party login. It is an independent community pedestrian-safety tool, not a government, police, emergency, medical, legal, or financial service. Emergencies and incidents in progress are directed to 911.

Core services are Apple MapKit, Core Location, PhotosUI, UIPasteboard, camera access, and ColumbiaWalks HTTPS endpoints at directus.rndtech.org/columbiawalks-api. The police-tip tool opens the official CRIMEWATCH site externally and has no path that submits a tip or media to CBPD. No Gmail credential or recipient address is in the app.

After upload, the server may request model-derived incident weather from Open-Meteo using only two-decimal incident coordinates and the incident date/hour. It sends no live phone location, precise coordinates, narrative, photo, contact, or report ID. The app contacts no weather service, adds no permission, and weather failure never blocks a report.
