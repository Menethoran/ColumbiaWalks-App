# ColumbiaWalks release policy

- The latest policy (September 28, 2026) classifies a version as internal testing only when its final dot-separated numeric component is exactly `0`. Examples: `3.17.0`, `3.17.1.0`, `3.17.10.0`, and `3.17.11.0` are internal; `3.17.1`, `3.17.10`, and `3.17.20` are public candidates. This uses the version string, not a platform build code. It supersedes the earlier patch-ending-in-digit-zero policy.
- Do not distribute internal versions through the public website, public GitHub releases, Play production/open/closed testing, external TestFlight, or the App Store. Run the executable distribution guard for the intended channel before staging artifacts.
- Current public candidate 3.17.1 must KEEP mandatory [TEST] markers between every word of its police-tip subject and message, including headings and placeholders. This feature-level testing requirement is independent of the release-channel version rule. Reapply markers to edits before a person submits the official form.
- The app prepares existing complaint details locally and fills only the verified Columbia Borough Police Department CRIMEWATCH form, selecting Anonymous and Other. It may dismiss the specific optional subscription notice. Never automate final submission, the agreement, CAPTCHA, or file attachment. Do not interpret autofill or popup dismissal as receipt.
- Normal website tabs cannot be filled or dismissed across origins. Keep the website handoff accurate about manual copy/paste and user submission. Do not proxy tip contents through ColumbiaWalks.
- Public website APK updates require the established original signing certificate. Never substitute a Play upload key or debug signer. Preserve historical 3.17.0 and 3.17.10 branches and artifacts.
- The historical 3.17.0 private test intake is separate. Do not add contact identifiers, account/installation identifiers, media, or public reads to it, and do not call it from the CRIMEWATCH flow.
- Preserve source/release baselines and record local QA, store upload, website publication, official submission, and confirmed receipt separately. iOS platform marketing versions remain three components; future four-component logical test versions require `CW_RELEASE_VERSION` with a compatible platform version and the same internal guard.
